    Your unfair advantage, while it lasts.
    Most people never push back — 61% of renters never negotiate at all
    — and that is exactly why pushing back works: it tells a landlord
    something about you they couldn't otherwise know. If enough people start
    doing this, landlords raise the opening number for everyone and the cost
    lands on the ones who stay quiet. That result survived our audit when most
    didn't. And the same trick runs on proof: showing a landlord something
    they can check beats saying it, right up until everyone does it, at which
    point saying nothing starts to look like having nothing. Both edges are
    real and both decay. So: use them while they're worth using, and know
    what they are.
