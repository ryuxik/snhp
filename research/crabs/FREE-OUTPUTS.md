# FREE-OUTPUTS — the Principle G register for the crab rent study

> **BUILT RETROSPECTIVELY, 2026-07-25. This is not what the register is for.**
>
> DESIGN-PRINCIPLES G rule 3 says the register is written *before* the run and
> published *with* the results, unedited — because "amending it afterwards is
> how a readout becomes a finding." This one was written after every run, every
> gate and every kill verdict already existed. It therefore has no power to
> constrain what this study claimed; it can only say, after the fact, which of
> those claims were entitled to be claims.
>
> It is filed anyway for three reasons: the study's results are still being
> written up, so it changes what the article may say; it is the worked example
> the principle was derived from; and the honest form of a rule discovered too
> late is to apply it to the case that produced it and publish the damage.
>
> Everything below is checked against `SPEC.md`, `SPEC-A2.md`, `PREREG.md` and
> `RESULTS.md`, and against the code. Parameter classes are the ones in
> `principles.py:PARAM_SOURCES`; this file is the output-by-output view of the
> same facts, which is the view that makes four of them visible at all.

## 1. The register

**FITTED** means the observable is pinned, directly or through any parameter, by
a number someone chose in order to reproduce it. Per G rule 1, indirection is
not laundering and two parameters fitted to two halves of one fact fit the whole
fact. A fitted observable is a **readout**: it may be reported, and it may not
be claimed as a result.

**FREE** means no parameter in the model was set by reference to it. Only these
may be claimed. Free is not the same as *sourced* — several free observables are
measured in units built entirely out of INVENTED constants, and that is noted
where it matters.

|---|---|---|---|

### The compounding cases, stated separately

G rule 1 exists because these are invisible one parameter at a time:

- **Retention (row 2).** `move_med` fits the rent-driven half of turnover;
  `p_exo_*` fits the non-rent half. Each disclosure is silent about the other.
  Together they leave the gate with nothing to test.
- **The counter rate (row 3).** `courage_med` fits the cost of asking to land on
  39%; `belief0` fits the perceived odds of winning to land on the 61%
  complement. Two ends of one split.
- **Vacancy (row 7).** `searcher_inflow` fits ~6% on the demand side; `V_TARGET`
  installs 6% on the pricing side. The same published aggregate twice.
- **Concession depth (row 8).** `DOM_LEARN` makes the reservation fall in
  days-on-market; `DOM_CUT` makes the ask fall in days-on-market. Two mechanisms
  for one relationship, and K22 measures that relationship.

## 2. Which validation gates were on fitted observables

G rule 2: **a validation gate on a fitted observable is not a test. It is an
identity.** Ten criteria were run across three gates. Four were free.

| gate | criterion | observable | free? | verdict as reported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **GATE 1** | **V1** counter success 0.15–0.30 | concession incidence | **FITTED** (`vacancy`, `face_premium`, `renewal_cap`) | FAIL |
| | **V2** retention 0.45–0.65 | retention | **FITTED — identity** (`p_exo_*` + `move_med`) | PASS |
| | **V3** tenure ratio ≥ 1.5× | tenure gradient | **FREE** | FAIL |
| **GATE 2** | **V4** mom zero-increase 10–30% | zero-increase share | **FREE** | FAIL |
| | **V5** mom concession rate ≤ 20% | concession incidence | **FITTED** (`vacancy`) | FAIL |
| | **V6** inst concession rate 15–35% | concession incidence | **FITTED** (`vacancy`) | FAIL |
| | **V7** inst push ≥ 3× mom push | renewal increase level | **FITTED** (`renewal_cap`) | FAIL |
| **GATE 3** | **V8** supply raises vacancy, new-let < sitting | vacancy response | **sign FREE, level FITTED** | PASS |
| | **V9** new-let negative beside renewal positive | renewal/new-let spread | **FREE** | PASS (3rd attempt, sign only) |
| | **V10** endogenous retention within 5pp of Phase 1 | retention | **FITTED ON BOTH SIDES** — `market.py` draws leave events from the same `p_exo` and `move_med`, and the *target* is Phase 1's own fitted retention | FAIL by 0.3pp |

**Six of ten criteria were on fitted observables. Two of the three gates had a
fitted majority.**

Three things follow, and none of them was visible to the per-parameter audit:

1. **V2 is the only criterion the study ever passed on the first attempt, and it
   is an identity.** RESULTS reports "V2 passes everywhere" in both
   specifications and on held-out seeds. It could not have done otherwise —
   PREREG §2 lists turnover and retention in a column headed *"Source"* with the
   words *"(calibration target)"* beside both, and then §3 makes retention V2.
   SPEC §8 was half-right — it said retention was "partly calibrated in, not
   predicted" — but "partly" understates it, because it names only `move_med`.
2. **SPEC §8's other claim is contradicted by SPEC §5.** §8: *"V1 … and V3 … are
   genuine out-of-sample predictions of the station's DP. No station parameter
   is set by reference to them."* But `v_m`, a station parameter in §5's own
   table, is justified by the 2026 concession-incidence statistic. V3's half of
   that sentence stands; V1's does not.
3. **V9 and V8's sign are the only gate results the study is entitled to talk
   about as tests.** They are also the only free criteria that passed. That
   coincidence is worth stating plainly rather than leaving to be noticed: the
   study passed the two criteria nothing was fitted to and failed the rest,
   which is a better account of it than "three gates failed."

**What is *not* rescued by any of this.** All three gates failed. G's own track
record note applies: the fitted criteria failing anyway is the only reason
nothing was overclaimed off them. A fitted gate that *passes* is where the
damage happens, and V2 is the one that passed.

## 3. Consequences for what may be claimed

Read this beside `RESULTS.md`; it does not replace any verdict there.

**Cannot be claimed as findings** (readouts of fitted parameters):

- the concession / counter-success *rate* at any value, in any regime — including
  the crab-flu run's 0.24 → 0.72, whose write-up explicitly compares itself back
  to the 39.7%-of-listings figure that `vacancy` was set from;
- retention or turnover at any level, and any statement of the form "the model
  reproduces observed retention";
- the +10.7% renewal push as a match to the world (A7 already withdrew the
  AI-migration claim built on it; this extends to the push itself);
- K20's 1.08× as a *measurement* — it is `move_med` over `turn_cost + vacancy`;
- K21's quartile gradient as anything but a restatement of the switching-cost
  lognormal (the *direction*, and the fact that the group is narrow, survive);
- K22 in either direction, which RESULTS already reports as undecided;
- the vacancy *level*, the market rent *level*, and E[remaining wait].

**May be claimed** (free, subject to their own caveats):

- V9's sign — the renewal/new-let inversion from deadline structure under
  symmetric information — magnitude explicitly excluded (−24% vs MAA's −7%);
- K24's ablation result, that the mechanism is **level and not shape** (13%);
- K25's monotone decay with the response clock — *the gradient, not the $645*;
- V3's and V4's failures, which are genuine negative results on free
  observables;
- K13/K14's engine ordering, and K3/K8's externality, with magnitudes read as
  multiples of `ask_frac` rather than as dollars;
- K26's null, remembering that `signal_enabled = False` made it structural.

## 4. How this file should have been used

Written before the first run, the table in §1 would have said: *concession rate
FITTED, retention FITTED, counter rate FITTED, renewal push FITTED*. Four of the
five things the study set out to explain would have been marked readouts on day
one, and GATE 1 would have been visibly a test of one criterion, not three.
That is a cheaper discovery than three gate attempts and six mechanisms.

The mechanical half of this check now lives in `principles.py:PARAM_SOURCES`
and is enforced by `test_every_module_constant_and_dataclass_field_is_classified`
— which fails if any constant in `world.py` or `market.py`, or any `Params` /
`MarketParams` field, is not classified. But the coverage test only guarantees
that every parameter has *an* answer to "where did this number come from". It
cannot tell you that the answer names one of your own outputs. **That reading
is output-by-output, it is this file, and it has to be done by a person before
the first run.**
